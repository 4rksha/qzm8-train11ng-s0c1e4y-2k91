Warm-Up Protocol

General warm-up — before every workout (5–10 min):

  • 5–10 min light cardio (treadmill, stairmaster, elliptical, bike)
  • 10 reps/side arm swings
  • 10 reps/side arm circles
  • 10 reps/side front-to-back leg swings
  • 10 reps/side side-to-side leg swings
  • 15 reps/side cable external rotation (optional)

Exercise-specific warm-up — based on warm-up sets listed for that exercise:

1 set: ~60% of working weight × 6–10 reps
2 sets: ~50% × 6–10, then ~70% × 4–6
3 sets: ~45% × 6–10, ~65% × 4–6, ~85% × 3–4
4 sets: ~45% × 6–10, ~60% × 4–6, ~75% × 3–5, ~85% × 2–4
